Privacy Policy

Last updated: July 2026

1. Information We Collect

When you interact with our forms (Contact, Booking, or Review Submission), we collect information that you voluntarily provide:

  • Personal Details: Full Name, Email Address, and Role/Company Name.
  • Consultation & Project Details: Preferred appointment date/time, selected packages, and project scope messages.
  • Review Submissions: Optional rating, testimonials, and public feedback comments.

2. How We Use Your Information

Your information is strictly used for legitimate business operations:

  • Responding to your inquiries and scheduling consultation calls.
  • Sending automated email notifications and booking confirmations via EmailJS.
  • Publishing approved client reviews and testimonials on our website via Supabase.
  • We never sell, rent, or trade your personal data to third-party marketers.

3. Third-Party Services

We utilize trusted third-party cloud services to power website functionality:

  • Vercel: Global hosting and serverless distribution.
  • EmailJS: Secure client notification email dispatch.
  • Supabase: Cloud database storage for public client testimonials.
  • Calendly: Embedded meeting scheduler.

4. Data Retention & Your Rights

You have the right to request access to, update, or permanently delete your personal information or submitted reviews from our system at any time. To exercise these rights, please contact us directly.

5. Contact Us

If you have any questions regarding this Privacy Policy or data handling, please submit an inquiry via our Contact Form.
